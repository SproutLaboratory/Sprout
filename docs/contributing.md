## 💖 Simple Ways to Support

### ⭐ Star the Repository
The easiest way to show your support — just click the **Star** button on GitHub.

### 📢 Share
Tell others about Sprout. Share the link on social media, forums, or with your friends.

### 🐛 Report Issues
If you find a bug, let us know by creating an Issue.

### 💬 Join Discussions
Help others by answering questions in Discussions.
